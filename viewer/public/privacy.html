Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Streamy processes, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Streamy web viewer and the Streamy desktop application (together, the "Service"). The Service is operated by the Streamy developer ("we", "us", "our").

In short: Streamy uses your basic Discord profile so people know who is streaming. Your screen and audio are sent live to your viewers and are not recorded or stored by us. We do not sell your data, show ads, or train AI models on your streams.

1. Information we process

Category What it includes Why
Discord account User ID, username, display name, and avatar, received when you sign in with Discord. We do not request your email, password, message content, or server list. To sign you in, show who is streaming, and authorize hosting.
Live stream The screen area, window, and audio you choose to share while a stream is running. To deliver your stream to the viewers you are sharing with.
Room data The generated room ID, the stream name and avatar, viewer counts, and the public viewer link. To list the stream in the viewer and let viewers join.
Technical data IP address and connection metadata processed by Cloudflare and Discord (sign-in) while a connection is open; basic operational logs such as connection times, room IDs, and bandwidth totals. To establish the connection, keep it secure, and diagnose failures.
Local device data Settings, logs, and a signed-in identity token stored by the desktop app on your computer. To remember your preferences and keep you signed in. This data stays on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: authenticate you, start and deliver streams, and show the stream in the viewer.
  • To keep the Service safe and working: enforce viewer limits, prevent abuse, and debug connection problems.
  • To communicate about the Service when necessary, for example by posting a watch invitation in the configured Discord channel when a stream starts.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (providing the Service you asked for), our legitimate interests (security, reliability, and abuse prevention), and consent where required.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not record, store, or archive your screen, audio, or stream content. Streams travel live over WebRTC, directly to viewers or through a configured TURN server; the signaling service only carries connection setup messages.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the viewer.
  • We do not use your stream or account data to train machine-learning models.

4. When information is shared

  • Viewers. Anyone with access to the stream can see the screen and audio you share, and may be able to capture or record it themselves.
  • Discord. Sign-in and the optional stream announcement run through Discord and are subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare. The viewer site and signaling service are hosted on Cloudflare, which processes network and request data to deliver the Service and protect it from abuse.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or if necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the public.

The streamer's own computer and network also handle the stream; if you are a viewer, your connection goes to the streamer's peer connection, or through a configured TURN server, as part of normal WebRTC operation.

5. Retention

  • Stream content: not retained; it exists only while it is being transmitted.
  • Room and lobby data: the public listing is removed when the stream ends, and the room's stored state is cleared when the room becomes empty.
  • Operational logs: kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Local app data and the signed-in token: kept on your device until you sign out, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

6. Security

Connections use TLS/HTTPS and WSS. Access to a stream requires a valid room capability, and hosting requires a verified Discord identity. No system is perfectly secure; do not share sensitive information on screen that you would not want a viewer to see.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • stop sharing at any time by stopping the stream in the desktop app;
  • sign out of the desktop app, which removes the local identity token;
  • ask us to delete the limited account and room data we hold.

To make a request, contact Theo on Discord: discord.com/users/277539638397370369. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account it concerns. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required in their country, and is not available to anyone below Discord's minimum age. If you believe a child has used the Service, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. International transfers

Discord and Cloudflare operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Those providers use their own safeguards for international transfers as described in their privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. If a change is significant, we will make that clear on the Service.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Contact Theo on Discord: discord.com/users/277539638397370369.
